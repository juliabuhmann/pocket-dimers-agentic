## Definitions

### 1. Entry selection
- **Method.** X-ray (any `_exptl.method` = X-RAY DIFFRACTION) needs `_refine.ls_d_res_high` ≤ 3.0 Å. If that is missing, `_reflns.d_resolution_high` is used.
  EM needs `_em_3d_reconstruction.resolution` ≤ 3.5 Å. NMR, neutron-only and other methods are dropped with skip reason `method`.
- **Protein chain.** At least one polypeptide entity with a SEQRES (canonical sequence) length of ≥ 30. Shorter polypeptides count as *peptides*:
  they are recorded but are never protein chains for assembly typing or MBS calls.
- Release date is the earliest `_pdbx_audit_revision_history.revision_date`.

### 2. Assembly
- Assembly = the first `_pdbx_struct_assembly` (normally "1"). It is generated by our own parser of `oper_expression`, including
  products such as `(1-60)(61-88)`, so each copy keeps its provenance (label_asym_id, operator).
  If no assembly is defined, the asymmetric unit is used (`assembly_id = "AU"`).
- Assemblies with more than 60 polymer chains are skipped as `huge_assembly`. This stands in for Marsh's exclusion of viral capsids, fibrils and helical assemblies.
- `pisa_agrees` is True when assembly 1 is `author_and_software_defined_assembly`, or when an author-only assembly 1 has a
  software-defined assembly with the same oligomeric count. It is None when there is no software assembly.
- `is_structural_genomics` is set from `_pdbx_SG_project`, or from "STRUCTURAL GENOMICS" in the keywords.
- Only the first model and the first altloc (`.` plus the lowest alt id) are used. Hydrogens are dropped.

### 3. Chain identity and assembly type
- Each protein chain gets a UniProt accession from SIFTS (auth chain). When a chain has several segments, the one with the largest coverage is used (`any_chimeric` is flagged).
  If SIFTS has no entry, the entry's own `_struct_ref_seq` segments are used instead (`uniprot_src = struct_ref`, FALLBACK quality).
  If neither exists, the identity key is the md5 of the entity sequence.
- The UniProt position of a residue is `SP_BEG + (label_seq_id − RES_BEG)` within the SIFTS segment.
- Assembly types: **monomer** = 1 protein chain; **homomer** = ≥ 2 protein chains that share one identity key; **heteromer** = ≥ 2 keys.
  `has_nucleic`, peptide-chain counts and `has_antibody` are also recorded. Antibody detection is a regex on the entity description: immunoglobulin, Fab, antibody, nanobody, VHH, scFv, Fv.

### 4. Ligand instances and classes
- **Instance.** A ligand instance is one non-polymer residue under one assembly operator. Exceptions:
  - A branched entity forms one instance per subchain.
  - Non-polymer residues joined by `struct_conn` covalent links are **merged** into one instance (union-find). `ccd_key` joins the component codes, and `ccd` is the largest component.
  - Water is excluded.
- **Covalent ligands.** A ligand with a covalent link to a polymer residue gets `is_covalent` and is kept. The partner residue types are stored.
- **Glycans.** `is_glycan` is set for a branched entity, or for a saccharide-type CCD that is covalently linked to protein. Glycans are flagged and excluded from the primary set.
- **Duplicates.** Copies of the same `ccd_key` whose centroids lie < 2.0 Å apart after the assembly operators are one physical ligand.
  The first copy is kept; the others get `is_dup_copy` and `dup_of`. This covers special positions and overlapping copies.
- **Symmetry groups.** `sym_group` is a hash of the CCD plus the multiset of (protein identity, UniProt position) over the contacted residues, so it ignores chain labels.
  Symmetry-equivalent sites in homomers therefore share an ID.
- **`ligand_classes.parquet`** has one row per CCD code (51,537) and can be revised without rerunning contacts.
  - It holds RDKit properties from the CCD SMILES (CACTVS canonical preferred): MW, cLogP, HBD/HBA, Ro5 violations, QED.
  - Classes are assigned by the first match in `CLASS_ORDER`:
    1. `metal_ion`: one heavy atom, a metal.
    2. `metal_cluster`: no carbon, more than one heavy atom, contains a metal (Fe-S and similar).
    3. `inorganic`: no carbon.
    4. `heme_porphyrin`: name regex, or the PDBe "Heme" class.
    5. `cofactor`: in the PDBe/CoFactor list.
    6. `nucleotide_like`: N-glycosidic base–furanose SMARTS, or a nucleotide CCD type.
    7. `sugar_glycan`: saccharide CCD type.
    8. `amino_acid`: peptide-linking CCD type.
    9. `lipid_fatty_acid`: ≥ 8 consecutive acyclic sp3 carbons.
    10. `drug_like`: either of
        - (primary) the UniChem-mapped ChEMBL max_phase is ≥ 1 and there are ≥ 12 heavy atoms;
        - (FALLBACK flag) Ro5 with ≤ 1 violation, QED ≥ 0.5 and ≥ 12 heavy atoms. `druglike_source` records which rule fired.
    11. `other_organic`.
  - The Marsh 3-class scheme (`marsh_class`) is: metal_ion → metal; cofactor, heme and metal_cluster → cofactor; everything else → small_molecule.
- **Additive / relevance labels.** Every label is stored as a column; no ligand rows are dropped.
  - `biolip_status` (primary) is matched on (pdb, CCD, ligand auth chain, ligand auth_seq) against BioLiP2:
    - `relevant`: present in BioLiP2.
    - `additive`: absent, and the entry's release date is ≤ the newest release date covered by BioLiP2 (cutoff is in `run_info.tsv`).
    - `not_covered`: the entry is newer than BioLiP2. These get `is_additive` = FALLBACK = (BioLiP-list geometric rule OR PLINDER), with `additive_label_source = FALLBACK_geom_plinder`.
  - `biolip_list_geom_artifact` is the BioLiP2 rule without the PubMed step: the code is on the list AND (more than 15 copies in the AU OR the site is only 2 consecutive residues).
    **Simplification:** we cannot run the PubMed title/abstract check. `biolip_list_strict_artifact` (on the list at all) is the opposite bound.
  - `plinder_artifact` = on the PLINDER badlist, OR caught by the generic filters (< 2 C or < 5 heavy atoms, |charge| > 2, an unbranched hydrocarbon chain > 12, or unknown atoms). Single non-CHNOPS atoms are treated as ions and exempt.
  - `is_ligand_of_interest` comes from `_pdbx_entity_instance_feature` "SUBJECT OF INVESTIGATION".

### 5. Contacts and MBS
- **Atom contact.** A ligand heavy atom and a polymer heavy atom are in contact if d ≤ vdW_i + vdW_j + 0.5 Å, using gemmi radii (C = 1.7 Å).
  A flat 4.0 Å cutoff is stored as a sensitivity variant. Each contacted residue stores `n_vdw` (atom pairs), `n_simple` and `min_dist`.
- **Binding residue.** Primary: ≥ 1 vdW atom pair (BioLiP 2013 / Marsh). BioLiP2 variant: ≥ 2 pairs (`*_biolip2` columns).
- **Bound.** A ligand is bound if it has ≥ 2 protein binding residues in total (`is_bound`, `is_bound_biolip2`, `is_bound_4A`).
- **MBS (primary, `is_mbs`).** At least 2 protein chains (≥ 30 aa) each contribute ≥ 1 binding residue. Variants:
  - `is_mbs_min2` and `is_mbs_min3`: ≥ 2 or ≥ 3 residues per chain.
  - `is_mbs_biolip2`.
  - `is_mbs_4A`.
  - `is_mbs_excl_antibody`.
  Per-chain counts are stored (`chain_counts*`), so any stricter rule can be applied later.
- **Homomer MBS descriptors.**
  - `mbs_contrib_same_entity` and `mbs_contrib_same_protein`.
  - `mbs_contrib_symmetry_copy`: two contributing chains come from the same label_asym under different operators (crystallographic symmetry), as opposed to NCS copies.
  - `on_axis_jaccard` / `is_on_axis`: Jaccard ≥ 0.5 between the UniProt residue sets contacted on the two top chains.
  - `chainpair_rot_angle` and `c2_axis_dist`: the two top chains are superposed by Kabsch on common CAs. If the rotation is 180 ± 15°, the distance from the ligand centroid to that axis is recorded.
- **Contact residue list (`contacts`).** Each entry has: chain (assembly chain id, e.g. `A` or `A-2`), auth_chain, label_asym, op, entity, kind (protein/peptide/nucleic), label_seq, auth_seq (with insertion code), comp, unp_acc, unp_pos, n_vdw, n_simple, min_dist.

### 6. Redundancy reduction
- `cluster30` and `cluster95` are the RCSB entity clusters of the dominant contacted chain (the chain with the most binding residues).
- `is_h1_rep_entry` and `is_h1_rep_instance` mark one representative per (cluster30 × CCD) within the primary set: best resolution, ties broken by the earliest ID.

### Primary set
`in_primary_set` = not a duplicate copy ∧ bound ∧ not a glycan ∧ not an additive (BioLiP2 primary label, or FALLBACK when not covered).

## Validation (v1 list; v2 adds 4 tests, see "v2 changes". 16 tests, all pass)
- **Toy synthetic assembly** (2-fold operator). Checks:
  - an MBS ligand on the axis (axis distance < 0.1 Å);
  - its symmetry copy flagged as a duplicate;
  - a distant ligand with no contacts (negative control);
  - a 1-residue ligand that is not bound;
  - a 2-consecutive-residue SBS site.
- **Contact-threshold unit test** at vdW sum + 0.5 ± 0.01 Å and at 4.0 Å. Operator-expression parser.
- **Selection:** 1D3Z (NMR) gives `method`; 5A40 (X-ray 3.6 Å) gives `resolution`.
- **1HVR** HIV-1 protease + XK2: homomer MBS (also min3 / BioLiP2 / 4 Å), NCS copies, on-axis, C2 axis distance 0.05 Å.
- **5CFL** STING + c-di-GMP (Marsh Fig. 1 MBS): MBS on-axis; the citrate copies are SBS.
- **1STP** streptavidin: 4 chains generated from 1 asym. Biotin is MBS by the any-residue rule, through Trp120 of the neighbouring subunit
  (the textbook contact). It is **not** MBS by min2. This is a crystallographic symmetry copy.
- **3PTB** trypsin: BioLiP2 marks BEN `additive` and CA `relevant`. **3Q92** (Marsh Fig. 1 SBS example): SO4 additive, XNB relevant.
  GOL, SO4, PEG and EDO are flagged by both the BioLiP list and PLINDER.
- **4HHB** haemoglobin: heteromer of 2 UniProts; 4 hemes, all SBS. PO4 (asym F) has no protein contact (real negative control).
- **1NEK** succinate dehydrogenase: heme b is a heteromer MBS between SdhC and SdhD (min2 as well); FAD is SBS.
- **1HEW** lysozyme + tri-NAG branched entity: flagged as a glycan.
- Class spot checks: ZN, HEM, FAD, ATP, C2E, NAG, SO4, SF4, PLM, NVP; indinavir counts as ChEMBL drug-like.

Observed caveat: in 3Q92 (Marsh's SBS example), one of the two XNB copies picks up Asp27 of the other chain at 2.76 Å.
So it is MBS by the any-residue rule and SBS by the ≥ 2-per-chain rule. Marsh's per-protein call evidently corresponds to the stricter reading or the AU-level view.

## Known limitations / simplifications
- BioLiP2 labels are AU-based and come from curation, so they carry noise. Examples: FOL in 1RB2 and FOK in 1CJK are absent from BioLiP2 and therefore become `additive`.
  The BioLiP2 PubMed check cannot be reproduced; we use the geometric part only, as a sensitivity flag.
- p66/p51 HIV RT, and other polyprotein-derived or processed chains that share one UniProt accession, are typed as **homomers** (e.g. 3HVT).
- The antibody regex is heuristic. "Fv" and "Fab" may also catch non-antibody descriptions.
- Assembly 1 is used rather than the PDBe preferred assembly.
- Assemblies with more than 60 chains are skipped rather than excluded by taxonomy (virus, fibril).
- The cofactor list is PDBe/CoFactor only (no ChEBI role). For example, ATP, ADP and NAD analogues outside the list fall into `nucleotide_like`.
- There is no metabolite / non-metabolite split yet; ChEBI or HMDB mapping is not done.
- The ChEMBL phase is a max over every ChEMBL ID mapped to a CCD code. Glycerol and citrate have phase 4, but they fall below the heavy-atom floor or are dropped by the additive labels.
- Altlocs: only the lowest alt id is kept. Microheterogeneity is otherwise not modelled.
- Residue-level UniProt mapping is segment arithmetic on the SIFTS chain TSV, not the residue-level SIFTS XML.

## v2 changes (CENSUS_VERSION = "v2")
These fix compliance with the frozen DESIGN, following the independent audit in `reviews/audit_part1_census.md` and the PI's v2 instructions.
- v2 outputs replace the v1 outputs in `data/part1/` and `results/part1/`.
- v1 is kept side by side in `data/part1/v1/` and `results/part1/v1/`.
- Every row carries a `census_version` column.

1. **Primary binding residue requires ≥ 2 atom contacts** (BioLiP2, DESIGN §0): `MIN_ATOM_CONTACTS_PRIMARY = 2`.
   - The v1 rule (≥ 1 atom pair) is kept as `*_1atom` columns. These replace v1's `*_biolip2` columns, which were the ≥ 2 rule.
   - `is_bound`, `is_mbs`, `is_mbs_min2/3`, `contacts`-derived site sets, the on-axis call and the two-consecutive-residue flag all follow the primary rule.
2. **SUBJECT OF INVESTIGATION arm.**
   - `is_relevant` = BioLiP2-relevant OR wwPDB `_pdbx_entity_instance_feature` SUBJECT OF INVESTIGATION.
   - `relevance_source` records which arm applies (biolip2 / soi / biolip2+soi).
   - `is_additive` = NOT `is_relevant` for covered entries.
3. **BioLiP2 coverage.**
   - `biolip_covered` = the entry was released ≤ the BioLiP2 snapshot (the newest release date among BioLiP2 entries; see `run_info.tsv`).
   - **The primary set is restricted to covered entries.**
   - Post-snapshot entries go into `in_postsnapshot_sensitivity_set`. Their additive label is FALLBACK (geometric BioLiP-list rule OR PLINDER), with SoI ligands kept.
   - Reason (audit H-3): the fallback removes Fe–S clusters and keeps Cl⁻, unlike BioLiP2.
4. **Symmetric-site collapse by tolerance.**
   - `site_group` is a union-find within each entry. It links same-`ccd_key` representative instances whose chain-agnostic (protein identity, UniProt position) residue sets have Jaccard ≥ 0.5 (`SITE_JACCARD_MIN`).
   - Duplicate copies inherit their representative's group.
   - The exact-hash `sym_group` is kept for comparison.
   - The collapse rate on 2-copy homodimers is reported in `site_collapse_homodimers.tsv`.
5. **Dedup before the on-axis call.**
   - All ligand altlocs are now read; v1 kept only the first. Each alt conformer is a copy, and non-first conformers are duplicates (`dup_reason = altloc`).
   - Same-CCD copies are duplicates if their centroids are < 2 Å apart (`centroid`), OR if > 50% of heavy atoms lie within 1.5 Å of the other copy's atoms (`atom_overlap`). The atom-overlap rule catches ligands disordered across a 2-fold, e.g. 1BM7 at 2.04 Å.
   - The representative's `contacts` are the **union** over its copies: per residue, max `n_vdw` and min distance. `n_merged_copies` counts the copies.
     All MBS and on-axis calls on representatives use the merged contacts.
   - On-axis primary is unchanged: Jaccard ≥ 0.5 of the UniProt residue sets on the two top chains.
   - New `is_on_axis_geom`: the centroid is ≤ 3 Å from the chain-pair C2 axis (rotation 180 ± 15°). Agreement between the two rules is in `homomer_mbs_geometry.tsv`.
   - Checked cases:
     - 2ROX: both T4 channels on-axis by both rules (v1 called channel 2 off-axis).
     - 1BM7: both channels have J = 1.0 and are geometric on-axis.
     - 1HVR: on-axis.
     - 3GRS FAD: off-axis by both rules, and the two copies form one site.
6. **Homomer typing.**
   - Chains of different entities count as the same protomer only if they share UniProt AND have ≥ 90% identity over ≥ 80% coverage. The alignment uses SIFTS UniProt positions.
   - **Deviation:** coverage is computed over the *longer* chain, not the shorter one. With shorter-chain coverage, p51 (a prefix of p66) would make HIV RT a homomer, which contradicts the required 3HVT outcome.
   - If all protein chains share one UniProt but the entities are not equivalent, the type is the new `same_uniprot_hetero` (3HVT p66/p51; thrombin light/heavy chains in 11RI and 3QTV).
     These are reported separately and excluded from the primary set. `has_same_uniprot_multi_entity` also flags such pairs inside heteromers.
7. **Photosynthesis flag.**
   - `is_photosynthetic` (entry level) is set when a ligand's CCD name matches `CHLOROPHYLL|PHEOPHYTIN` (derived from components.cif, no hard-coded list), or when the entry keywords match `PHOTOSYSTEM|LIGHT-HARVESTING|PHOTOSYNTHE|REACTION CENT`.
   - The table set `primary_excl_photosynthetic` is a sensitivity.
8. The drug-like definition is unchanged. The analysis agent builds ChEMBL flags separately.

New tests: 2ROX on-axis after dedup, 1BM7 altloc dedup, 3HVT `same_uniprot_hetero`, 3GRS off-axis, and 1RB2 folate (SoI arm).
The 1RB2 test is conditional: the entry has no SoI record, so FOL stays additive. The toy assembly now uses 2-atom ligand positions so that the ≥ 2-contact rule applies.
