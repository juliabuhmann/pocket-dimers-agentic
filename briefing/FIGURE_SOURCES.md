# Figure sources (briefing)

All figures are generated by `briefing_src/make_figures.py`:

```
.venvs/briefing/bin/python briefing_src/make_figures.py        # all figures
.venvs/briefing/bin/python briefing_src/make_figures.py 5 7    # a subset
```

Output: `briefing_src/figures/*.png` at 200 dpi. These figures are a first draft. They have been rendered and checked by eye for layout, but nobody other than the producing agent has yet confirmed the plotted values against the report.

Colour roles are the same in every figure: blue = with partner / dimer model; orange = isolated or single chain; grey = random null or control; yellow = drug-like; green = cofactor; violet = homomer; aqua = heteromer; dark grey = experimental PDB sites. The colour sets were checked with the dataviz-skill palette validator. Each series also has its own marker shape or a direct label.

Where REPORT.md quotes a verifier's number and the producer table differs, the script reads the verifier's file. These cases are marked "verifier" below.

## fig1_agentic_workflow.png: how the study was run

- **Data:** no results data.
  - Counts come from files: `reviews/r1_*.md`, `r2_*.md`, `audit_*.md`, `verify_*.md`, `report_review_*.md` and `retro_*.md` (globbed).
  - The commit count and wall clock come from `git` up to the last commit touching `RESEARCH_TREE.md`, with the start time from `START.txt`.
  - The number of hypotheses (5) and dimer models (10,610) are parsed from `RESEARCH_TREE.md`.
- **Content:**
  - Boxes and arrows run from the human prompt to the AI PI agent, then literature/recon, design reviews (2 rounds, 7 reports) and the frozen design.
  - Four parallel producer agents follow, then the audit and verifiers (1 audit and 7 verification reports), report reviews (2) and the report.
  - A dashed arrow shows fixes and reruns going back to the producers.
  - Footer: 113 commits, ~17.5 h from the start to the final milestone commit, and 1 retrospective file.
- **Caption draft:** A human prompt was turned into a pre-registered design, analyses and a report by a team of AI agents. A separate set of AI reviewers and verifiers checked the design, re-derived the headline numbers and reviewed the report. Every claim in the report is tagged by whether an independent agent reproduced it.

## fig2_concepts.png: key concepts (cartoon, no data)

- **Data:** none.
- **(a)** The same two-chain complex is drawn twice. In the single-chain site (SBS) the ligand contacts only chain A. In the multi-chain site (MBS) it sits in the cleft between A and B.
- **(b)** A homodimer viewed down its twofold axis (lens symbol). An on-axis site holds one ligand on the axis. Off-axis sites come as two symmetry-related copies.
- **(c)** In a predicted dimer, a pocket forms at the interface. Removing the partner leaves no pocket in the isolated chain (a "dimer-only" pocket).
- **Caption draft:** Ligand sites can be formed by one protein chain (SBS) or by several (MBS). In symmetric homodimers, an MBS can lie on the twofold axis (one ligand shared by both copies) or off it (two copies). Some pockets in predicted dimers exist only when the partner chain is present.

## fig3_pdb_census.png: multi-chain fraction in the PDB

- **Data:** `results/part1/analysis/mbs_fraction_descriptives_seed0.tsv` (producer). The site and unit fractions were re-derived in `reviews/verify_v3.md` and agree to within 0.3 pp.
- **(a)**
  - MBS fraction of non-redundant units (30% sequence cluster × ligand), after the additive filter, by ligand class, for homomers and heteromers.
  - Error bars are the cluster-bootstrap 95% CIs from the table.
  - The dashed line is the all-oligomer unit fraction (30.6%).
- **(b)**
  - MBS fraction before vs after removing additives (BioLiP2 relevance / "subject of investigation").
  - Shown at site level (symmetry-collapsed) and unit level, for all oligomers, homomers and heteromers, with 95% CIs.
- **Caption draft:** About a quarter to a third of ligand sites in protein oligomers span more than one chain. Lipids and hemes are the most multi-chain-prone ligand classes and metal ions the least. Removing crystallisation additives barely changes the overall fraction (sites 26.7% → 26.3%).

## fig4_ligand_classes.png: pre-registered ligand-class tests (family A)

- **Data:**
  - `results/part1/analysis/hypothesis_tests.tsv`, rows: H1 primary, H1 strict_mbs, H2 primary, H3 primary (unit-level OR 35.4).
  - Verifier: `reviews/verify_part1/out/h3_stress.tsv`, row "presence-only", for the H3 cluster-level OR 13.8 (6.7–28.6), which REPORT.md quotes as "≈14". The same file's "primary" row gives the cluster counts.
  - The counts are confirmed in `reviews/verify_part1_results.md` §3a.
- **(a)** Forest plot of odds ratios with 95% CIs on a log scale. Filled diamonds are pre-registered primaries; open circles are sensitivity or descriptive estimates.
- **(b)** Drug-like vs cofactor composition of homomer MBS clusters: 64 vs 8 on-axis, 334 vs 576 off-axis.
- **Caption draft:** The pre-registered cofactor-vs-drug-like contrast (H1) is null, and the drug-like occupancy excess at MBS (H2) is weak. Sites on the homomer twofold axis are dominated by drug-like ligands (64 vs 8 clusters; cluster-level OR ≈14). Because the on-axis cofactor cell holds only 8 clusters, H3 is reported descriptively.

## fig5_recovery.png: do predicted dimers recover real multi-chain sites? (H4)

- **Data:**
  - `results/part2/h4/v2/hit_rates.tsv`, filter = filtered, lig_set = organic, conditions dimer, iso and dimer_null0. The random-patch null is the seed-0 dimer-side null, which is what REPORT §4.1 quotes.
  - Verifier: `reviews/verify_h4/stats_results.tsv`, rows "primary, unstratified cluster bootstrap (producer clusters)" and "family-weighted (each cluster weight 1 per class)". REPORT.md quotes the verifier CI (−0.006 to 0.338) rather than the producer's (−0.015 to 0.332) in `h4_tests.tsv`.
- **(a)** Hit rates (DCC ≤4 Å among the top n+2 pockets) for dimer models, isolated chains and random surface patches, for homo/hetero × MBS/SBS. Error bars are cluster-bootstrap 95% CIs.
- **(b)** Difference-in-differences (MBS gain minus SBS gain) with 95% CIs, site-weighted (pre-registered primary) vs family-weighted (exploratory), with a zero line.
- **Caption draft:**
  - Pockets predicted on AlphaFold dimer models recover experimental multi-chain ligand sites more often than pockets on the isolated chains (homodimers 0.40 vs 0.28), while single-chain sites show no such gain.
  - The pre-registered site-weighted test does not exclude zero (DiD +0.16, CI −0.01 to 0.34).
  - Weighting each protein family equally gives a consistent positive effect (exploratory).

## fig6_pocket_chemistry.png: interface pockets, predicted vs real

- **Data:**
  - Predicted (blue): `results/part2/q22/q22_props.tsv` (stratum all, seed 0), interface vs single-chain pockets.
  - PDB (dark grey): volume, charged and hydrophobic fractions from `results/part1/pocket_descriptors/pdb_vs_afdimer_pattern.tsv`, contrast "PDB MBS vs SBS ligand sites" (uncropped, merged-pocket volume).
  - PDB burrow_score is the pre-registered H2-secondary located-pocket value from `results/part1/pocket_descriptors/mbs_vs_sbs_cliffs_delta.tsv` (all_weighted, seed 0; δ −0.009). The merged-pocket burrow_score (+0.025) is not plotted.
  - fpocket druggability: `results/part1/fpocket/pdbsite_mbs_vs_sbs_cliffs_delta.tsv` (fp_drug, all_weighted).
  - Verification: uncropped deltas and fpocket in `reviews/verify_v3.md`; the Q2.2 deltas in `reviews/verify_q22.md`.
- **Content:** Cliff's δ with 95% CIs for five descriptors. There is no AF-dimer fpocket contrast of the same form; only a 114-pocket paired comparison exists, so it is not plotted.
- **Caption draft:** In predicted dimers, interface pockets are larger, more charged and less hydrophobic than single-chain pockets. Real ligand-bound multi-chain sites in the PDB are only modestly larger, with unchanged chemistry and no difference in fpocket druggability. Interface location alone therefore does not indicate a more druggable site.

## fig7_cofolding.png: AlphaFold3 co-folding with and without the partner

- **Data:**
  - On-axis drugs pooled over 3 seeds: `results/cofold/onaxis/seeds_summary.tsv` (pooled, metric sym), 21/36 vs 8/36. The counts are verified in `reviews/verify_v3.md`.
  - Off-axis cofactors (5/6 vs 6/6) and dimer-only active sites (6/8 vs 6/8), seed 0: verifier `reviews/verify_cofold/rescored_seed0.tsv` (success column). The producer metric gives 5/8 for the dimer-only single chain; REPORT.md uses the verifier's 6/8.
  - Heterodimer MBS: `results/cofold/hetero_ext/summary.tsv` (subset all), 14/26 vs 8/26.
  - Scatter: verifier ligand RMSDs, seed 0, from `rescored_seed0.tsv` (on-axis, off-axis, SBS control, dimer-only and H4 homodimer cases) and `results/cofold/hetero_ext/paired.tsv` (`rmsd_ver_*`, 26 heterodimer pairs).
- **(a)** Correct poses (ligand RMSD ≤2 Å) with vs without the partner chain, with counts above the bars.
- **(b)** Paired per-case ligand RMSD (single chain on x, dimer on y; log axes) with a diagonal and 2 Å lines, coloured and shaped by batch. Points beyond 60 Å are clipped to the edge.
- **Caption draft:**
  - When the partner chain is included, AF3 places drugs that bind on the twofold axis correctly more often (21/36 vs 8/36 over three seeds), and heterodimer-site ligands get lower RMSD.
  - Cofactors in off-axis or dimer-only active sites are placed equally well from one chain.
  - These are small case series (6–26 cases per batch). Memorisation of pre-2021 complexes may contribute.

## fig8_verification.png: what independent checks changed

- **Data:** text only. Each row's claim is checked by the script: it asserts that a quoted phrase occurs in the cited file (`require_text`), or the build fails.
  - `reviews/audit_part1_census.md`: 75% of drug-like labels came from the fallback rule; the symmetry collapse failed for 43% of copies.
  - `reviews/verify_part1_results.md`: H3 cluster-level OR ≈14; HIV PR contributes 127 units.
  - `reviews/verify_h4.md`: the permutation null is centred at 0.091.
  - `reviews/verify_h5.md`: 1.09 [0.97, 1.23] after amino-acid adjustment.
  - `reviews/verify_misc.md`: crop at 24.4% / 17.8% of sites.
  - `REPORT.md` and `reviews/verify_v3.md`: volume δ +0.107 → +0.071.
  - `reviews/verify_cofold.md`: 6/8 instead of 5/8; 109/112 calls agree.
  - `reviews/report_review_pi.md`: H1 reframed.
- **Content:** a table with one row per catch: which check, what it caught, and what changed in the analysis or report.
- **Caption draft:** Independent AI auditors and verifiers, who did not reuse the producers' code, caught labelling errors before any hypothesis test, an invalid permutation p-value, a cropping bias and over-stated effects. Each catch led to a fix, a rerun or a more cautious claim in the final report.
